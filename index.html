Constitutional Due Process

Let the Constitution work — not political hysteria.

This public signature campaign supports constitutional due process, fairness, and the right of every elected public official to answer accusations through lawful democratic institutions instead of trial by propaganda, outrage, and coordinated political attacks.

PROCESS

Due process matters

Impeachment should never become a shortcut for political warfare. Allegations must be tested fairly, evidence must be examined properly, and constitutional safeguards must apply equally to everyone.

FAIRNESS

Democracy requires restraint

Public anger alone cannot replace constitutional procedure. The stability of democratic institutions depends on fairness, transparency, and respect for lawful processes.

ORDER

Accountability includes fairness

Supporting due process does not automatically mean declaring innocence. It means allowing proper institutions to evaluate evidence without mob pressure.

“Justice is not protected when public opinion becomes the verdict before the process even begins.”

Why this signature matters
